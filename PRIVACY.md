# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the RevealFolio mobile application ("RevealFolio", the "app") handles information. In this policy, "RevealFolio", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the app and its developer.

If you have questions, contact us at **RevealFolio@gmail.com**.

---

## Summary

- **Your projects stay on your device.** Your photos, project details and business profile are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We have no servers, no user accounts and no cloud database, so we never receive this content.
- **Ads (free version).** The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and similar data to show and measure ads. You can limit this in your device settings.
- **Optional location features (Pro).** Location is used only when you choose a location feature. Only a city and state/region are ever saved.
- **When you email us,** we receive what you send.
- **We do not sell your personal information,** and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

---

## 1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is created by you and stored in the app's private storage on your device. **We do not collect, receive or have access to it.**

- **Photos.** Photos you take with the camera or choose from your gallery, including the full original image (kept so you can re-crop later) and the cropped version. Photos taken with a phone camera may contain embedded information (EXIF metadata) such as the date, camera model and, if your camera saves it, the location where the photo was taken. RevealFolio does not read or display this metadata, but it remains inside the stored original photo.
- **Project details.** Project names, categories, tags, notes, materials, and a location limited to city and state/region.
- **Business profile (Pro features).** Information you enter for your digital business card, such as business name, professional title, bio, specialties, phone number, email address, website, social media handles and logo.
- **Settings.** Preferences such as theme, export style templates, Kiosk Mode timer and an optional Kiosk Mode PIN.
- **Exported images.** Images you export are created on your device at the moment you export and saved to a temporary file for sharing. They leave your device only if you choose to share them.

You can view, edit or delete this information at any time within the app.

## 2. Information Processed by Third Parties

### 2.1 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of RevealFolio displays ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's **advertising ID**;
- your **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location;
- **device and app information**, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
- **ad interaction data**, such as ads viewed or tapped; and
- **diagnostic data** related to ad performance.

The app requests personalized ads where they are available, meaning ads may be tailored using data Google has associated with your advertising ID. Google's use of this data is governed by its own policies. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Your ad choices:** On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings (typically **Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**). After you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

Ads are never shown in Kiosk Mode. If paid features are offered and you purchase an ad-free option, ads will not be shown to you.

### 2.2 Location Features (Optional, Pro)

RevealFolio uses location only when you actively choose to:

- **"Use GPS" button.** When you tap it, the app asks for permission to access your device's **approximate** location. If you allow it, your coordinates are sent to your device's geocoding service (on Android, provided by Google) to look up the nearest city and state/region. **Only the city and state/region are saved.** Coordinates are never saved, and street addresses, postal codes and precise locations are never stored.
- **City search.** When you type in the location field, the text you type is sent to the **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** service to suggest matching cities. OpenStreetMap receives your search text and your IP address, as with any web request. See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

The app does not track your location in the background and never requests location automatically.

### 2.3 When You Contact Us

If you use **Report a Problem** or **Send Feedback**, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself. When you do, we receive:

- your email address and name as shown by your email provider;
- the content of your message and any attachments you add (such as screenshots); and
- for problem reports, the technical details shown on the report screen before you send: app version, free or Pro mode, theme, device model and operating system version.

We use this information only to respond to you and to fix problems or improve the app. We keep support emails for as long as needed to resolve your request, and delete them no later than **two years** after our last contact unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

### 2.4 Sharing You Choose

When you export an image, share a business card, or share a backup file, the content is sent to the app or service you select (for example a social media app, messaging app, email or cloud storage). That service's own privacy policy applies to what you share. We do not receive a copy.

### 2.5 Google Play

RevealFolio is distributed through Google Play. Google processes information about your download and, if paid features are offered, your purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.

## 3. What We Do Not Do

- We do not operate servers or user accounts, and we do not upload your photos or projects.
- We do not include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
- We do not access your contacts, calendar, microphone or files other than the photos you choose.
- We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data Google collects through AdMob, as described in Section 2.1, we do not share personal information with third parties.

## 4. App Permissions

RevealFolio asks for the following permissions only when needed. You can change them at any time in your device settings.

| Permission | Why it's used |
|---|---|
| Camera | To take before and after photos when you choose "Take Photo". |
| Photos and media | To let you choose photos from your gallery. |
| Approximate location | Only when you tap "Use GPS" (Pro), to fill in your city and state/region. |
| Notifications | To show backup progress and completion. These are generated on your device; we do not send push notifications. |
| Internet | To load ads and to search for city names. |

## 5. Backups

- **In-app backups.** RevealFolio can create backup files containing your project data and photos, including the original photos and any metadata embedded in them (see Section 1). Backups are saved where you choose. A backup saved to a folder you pick on your device may remain after you uninstall the app. Sharing a backup file shares everything in it.
- **Android Auto Backup.** Depending on your device settings, Android may back up app data to your Google Account. This backup is provided and controlled by Google. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

- **On your device:** Information stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private storage.
- **Backups:** Backup files you saved outside the app, and any Android Auto Backup data, are not removed automatically. Delete them yourself in your file manager or Google Account settings.
- **Support emails:** See Section 2.3.
- **Ad data:** Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

## 7. Security

Your information is stored in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your device with a screen lock. The Kiosk Mode PIN is a convenience to discourage casual exits while the app is on public display. It is not a security feature.

## 8. Children's Privacy

RevealFolio is intended for users aged **13 and older** and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us at RevealFolio@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Because your projects are stored only on your device, you can access, change or delete them directly in the app at any time.

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California and other U.S. states, you may have the right to:

- access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you (in practice, support emails you sent us);
- receive a copy of that information;
- object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent you have given;
- opt out of targeted advertising (see Section 2.1); and
- lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email RevealFolio@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. For data collected by Google through AdMob, you can also use Google's controls and your device's ad settings.

Where the law of the EEA or UK applies, we process support emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to you, and location features on the basis of your choice to use them.
