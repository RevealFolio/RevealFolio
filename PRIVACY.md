# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 22, 2026

### 1. Introduction
Welcome to RevealFolio. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application. RevealFolio is designed with a local-first architecture to respect your privacy; therefore, the application processes and operates your data entirely on your local device.

### 2. Data Processing and Storage
* **Photos and Media:** RevealFolio accesses your device's photo gallery or camera solely to allow you to import, crop, and display before-and-after images within the application. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any external servers.
* **User Input:** All text data you enter—including project names, tags, notes, and categories—is saved exclusively in your device's local storage. This data is never transmitted to external servers.
* **Local Backups:** The app provides a local backup feature that packages your project data and photos into a backup file stored on your device. When you choose to share a backup file using the app's built-in share function, the content is transmitted to the destination you select (such as cloud storage, email, or another app). The developer has no access to backup files you share, and the handling of that data is governed by the service you choose to share it with.

### 3. Third-Party Services
RevealFolio integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data, including your device's Advertising ID and app usage metrics, to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
* For comprehensive information on how Google handles this data, please review [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 4. Data Deletion
Because RevealFolio does not utilize user accounts, cloud databases, or centralized server storage, you maintain control over your information. Uninstalling the application from your device removes your locally stored data.

Please note that Android devices may retain some app settings through Android Auto Backup (Google's built-in device backup service). To ensure complete removal of all app data, you can disable Auto Backup for RevealFolio in your Android device settings before uninstalling.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective immediately upon posting the revised text. Your continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of those changes.
